My Story

A calling to walk beside others

"You don't have to do this thing called life alone."

Life is a journey filled with unexpected turns, difficult seasons, and moments when the next step is hard to see. Along the way, many people quietly carry burdens they believe they must face by themselves. On a Journey, But Not Alone exists to remind them that they don't have to.

This ministry was placed on my heart many years ago. I believe it is part of my calling from God: to help people find encouragement, support, and hope as they navigate life. My purpose is to walk alongside others—not to promise that every challenge will disappear, but to offer a listening ear, compassionate support, and a reminder that even in hard times, they are not forgotten or alone.

Faith can bring comfort and strength, especially when life feels uncertain. Through On a Journey, But Not Alone, I hope to create a welcoming space where people can feel seen, supported, and encouraged to keep moving forward. Every person's journey is different, and no one should have to pretend they have everything figured out.

The message at the heart of this ministry is simple: You don't have to do this thing called life alone. With God's guidance and the care of others, it is possible to find strength for today and hope for the road ahead.

— Rodney Davis, Founder
