# On a Journey, But Not Alone - Website (GitHub Pages)

## Project
- Ministry site for Rodney Davis, Austin, TX. Faith-based peer support,
  advocacy, spiritual guidance (for those who welcome it), essential items.
- Repo lives in the org `onjourneybutnotalone`. Rodney owns it; Andres builds.
- Live at https://onjourneybutnotalone.github.io (custom domain later, bought in Rodney's name).
- Keep costs at zero: plain HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, no paid services.

## Tech rules
- Static HTML only. Each page is self-contained, styles in the page or one shared css file.
- Images go in /assets. No stock-site screenshots; use Rodney's own photos/logo when he sends them.
- Mark every missing fact with `TODO:` in the HTML. Never invent phone numbers,
  addresses, hours, nonprofit status, or church affiliation.
- Do not publish Rodney's email or phone until he confirms what's public.
- Rodney's story text stays in his own words; light cleanup only.
- Must work on phones. Keep the 988 crisis line in the footer.
